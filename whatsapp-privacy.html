WhatsApp Assistant — Privacy & Data Deletion

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Operator: PLUS GOLD - ENERGY & INVESTMENTS Ltd. (company no. 51-231603-5), operating the Super Star Plus supermarket, Main Road, Majd al-Krum, Israel · +972-4-9076772 · info@superstar-plus.co.il

1. What the assistant does

Customers who message the store's WhatsApp number receive automatic answers to general questions — opening hours, promotions, products and the customer club. The assistant uses the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). Our staff keep answering from the store's WhatsApp Business app; when a staff member replies, or a customer asks for a person, the assistant stays silent with that customer for a while.

2. What information is processed

  • The customer's WhatsApp phone number / ID and profile name, as provided by WhatsApp.
  • The text of the messages exchanged with the assistant.
  • Images, voice notes and files are not processed — the assistant replies with one short message and a staff member follows up.

The assistant does not have access to customer-club accounts, purchases or ID numbers, and does not ask for them. Please do not send card details or passwords in the chat.

3. Why it is used

Only to answer the customer's question. The messages are not used for advertising, profiling or marketing, and are not sold.

4. Who processes it for us

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp) — delivers the messages.
  • Anthropic — an AI service that drafts the answer from the message.
  • Supabase — our cloud database and server.

These providers act on our behalf, under confidentiality and data-security obligations. Some of them store data outside Israel (for example in the EU or the US).

5. How long it is kept

  • The latest messages of a conversation are kept for up to 24 hours, only so the assistant can follow the conversation, and are then deleted automatically.
  • A short "pause" record (the WhatsApp ID and an end time, up to 7 days) is kept while the assistant is silent with a customer, and is deleted when the pause ends.
  • A daily total of the number of answers (without any personal details) is kept for budgeting.

6. Data deletion

Conversation data is deleted automatically within 24 hours, without any action on your part. To have your data deleted sooner, to ask what we hold about you, or to stop receiving automatic answers, contact us at info@superstar-plus.co.il or +972-4-9076772 with the phone number you used. We delete it and confirm within 30 days at the latest.

Messages also remain in your own WhatsApp app and in the store's WhatsApp Business app, as with any WhatsApp conversation; you can delete them from your phone at any time.
